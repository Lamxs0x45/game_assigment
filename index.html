MEMORY CHALLENGE

How well do you know your classmates?

Test your memory and get to know your classmates and teachers at VIA University College. Flip the cards, find matching faces and complete the challenge!

80 Cards
40 Pairs
1 Challenge
How to play:
  1. Click on a card to flip it over.
  2. Click on another card to find a match.
  3. Matching pairs remain open. Incorrect pairs turn face down again
  4. Try to find all the matching pairs!
